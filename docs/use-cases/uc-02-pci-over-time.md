# UC-02 Track PCI over time

## Actor
- Primary: pavement management engineer; airport pavement management staff.
- Secondary: Continuum consultant.

## Goal
See how a section or a set of sections deteriorated between surveys, to time M&R and support condition reports.

## Example question
- "How has the PCI of section X changed since 2018?"
- "Which sections lost the most PCI points since the previous survey?"

The year (2018) is an example.

## Data required
- Section
- PCI
- Year

## Expected result
- PCI for each survey year and the change in PCI points between surveys, with the source rows.
- A table of section, survey year, PCI and change in PCI points. Downloadable.
- The answer states the years compared.
- The answer notes the years in which a section has no survey, instead of filling the gap.
