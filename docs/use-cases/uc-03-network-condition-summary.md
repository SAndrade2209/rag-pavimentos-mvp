# UC-03 Summarize network condition for budget and reporting

## Actor
- Primary: pavement management engineer; airport pavement management staff.

## Goal
Produce network-level condition figures for funding requests and condition reports.

## Example question
- "What was the average PCI of the network in each survey year since 2020?"
- "How many sections were below the threshold in 2024 compared with 2022?"

The years (2020, 2022, 2024) are examples.

## Data required
- Section
- PCI
- Year

## Expected result
- Averages or counts by survey year, with the table they were computed from. Downloadable.
- The answer states that averages are by section count, not weighted by area or length, because those fields are not in the phase 1 data.
- For counts below a threshold, the answer states the threshold used (same rule as UC-01).

## Open items
- Area or length per section would allow area- or length-weighted PCI. Whether the datasets include them is not known.
- PCI threshold source: per-client setting, stated by the user, or both. Not decided.
