# UC-04 Find sections by location to plan inspections

## Actor
- Primary: district/maintenance staff; airport pavement management staff.

## Goal
Find sections near a point or inside an area, with their latest PCI and last survey year, to decide which sections to survey next or which records to verify.

## Example question
"Which sections within 2 km of these coordinates have not been surveyed since 2021?"

The radius (2 km) and the year (2021) are examples.

## Data required
- Section
- Coordinates
- PCI
- Year

## Expected result
- A table of section, latest PCI, last survey year and distance from the point. Downloadable.
- The answer states the search radius and the coordinates used.

## Open items
- Coordinate format (one point per section or line geometry) and coordinate reference system. Not known.
